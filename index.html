Golden Rules of Waste Segregation

✅ The Essential DOs

  • • Rinse all food residue: Wash milk sachets, oil pouches, and plastic yogurt cups with a splash of water before putting them in the dry recycling bin.
  • • Keep separate dustbins: Place at least two dustbins in the kitchen (Green for wet food, Blue for dry packaging).
  • • Wrap sanitary waste safely: Wrap baby diapers and pads in old newspapers and mark with a red cross (X) so sanitation workers are forewarned.
  • • Compost at home: Turn vegetable scraps and tea bags into black gold for your balcony plants.

❌ The Critical DON'Ts

  • • Never mix wet & dry waste: Once clean cardboard or paper is wet with rotting vegetable liquids, it can no longer be recycled.
  • • Never burn plastic or fallen leaves: Burning garbage releases toxic dioxins and carcinogens into our neighborhood air.
  • • Don't flush wet wipes or plastic: They do not break down in sewage pipes and cause major urban storm drain blockages.
  • • Don't dump batteries into normal bins: Heavy metals like lithium and lead seep into drinking aquifers. Always hand them over to E-Waste collection.
